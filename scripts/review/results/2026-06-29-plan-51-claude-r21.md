The plan explicitly condemns this for #63 — line 228: "#63 consumes this config rather than being parsed by #51 CI through brittle plan-prose matching" — yet adopts it for the manifest matrix. Internal inconsistency; brittle. Impact is bounded because line 268–269 makes the #51 matrix authoritative on disagreement, so I rate it MAJOR-leaning-MINOR rather than a hard blocker, but the test as specified is fragile.
